# Home address and emergency contacts

Synthetic policy for demonstration. Not a real employer's policy.

## Keeping the home address current

Employees update their home address within 30 days of moving. The HR Assistant can make
the change: it shows the exact new address and applies it only after the employee
confirms. The change takes effect immediately in the employee record.

The home address matters for three things:

- State tax withholding follows the home address. A move to another state changes
  withholding starting with the next pay period that begins after the change. Employees
  who live and work in different states may need an additional state form; the payroll
  team contacts them when one is required.
- The annual W-2 is mailed to the address on file on December 31.
- Benefits mail and plan notices go to the address on file.

A mailing address different from the home address is set by opening an HR ticket in the
profile domain.

## Emergency contacts

Every employee keeps at least one emergency contact on file with a name, relationship,
and a phone number. The HR Assistant can change the primary emergency contact with the
same confirm step as an address change. A second emergency contact is added through an HR
ticket. Emergency contacts are used only when an employee is injured or cannot be reached
during a safety event.
