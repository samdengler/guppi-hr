# Contacting HR and HR tickets

Synthetic policy for demonstration. Not a real employer's policy.

The HR Assistant answers policy questions and makes a small set of changes directly:
home address, primary emergency contact, and direct deposit account. Everything else goes
to a person on the HR team through a ticket.

## Opening a ticket

An employee can ask the HR Assistant to open a ticket at any time, for example by asking
to talk to someone. The ticket records a short summary and a domain (profile, pay,
travel, or general), and the assistant gives the ticket id, which starts with HR-.

## Response times

- The HR team follows up on a ticket within two business days.
- Pay tickets about missing pay are handled the same business day.
- Safety and harassment concerns are not handled through tickets: employees call the
  24 hour ethics line or contact their leader directly.

## What the HR Assistant does not do

The assistant does not change job, salary, leave balances, or benefits enrollment, and it
does not see medical information. Those requests go through a ticket.
