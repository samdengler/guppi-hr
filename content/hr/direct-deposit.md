# Direct deposit

Synthetic policy for demonstration. Not a real employer's policy.

Pay is deposited to one checking or savings account at a US bank. The HR Assistant shows
the account on file by its last four digits and can change it: it shows the new bank
routing number, account type, and the last four digits of the new account, and applies
the change only after the employee confirms.

## When a change takes effect

A change confirmed by 11:59 pm Eastern on the Wednesday before a Friday pay date applies
to that pay date. A change confirmed later applies to the following pay date, and that
Friday's pay goes to the previous account.

## Protecting the account

- Full account numbers are never stored in the employee record; only the last four
  digits are kept.
- HR never asks for a full account number by phone or email. A request to confirm an
  account number outside the HR Assistant or the payroll portal is a phishing attempt and
  is reported to security.
- After any change the employee receives a notice at the email on file. An employee who
  did not make the change opens an HR ticket in the pay domain right away.

## Split deposits

Splitting pay across up to three accounts is available by opening an HR ticket in the pay
domain; the HR Assistant manages the primary account only.
