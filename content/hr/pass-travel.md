# Pass travel privileges

Synthetic policy for demonstration. Not a real employer's policy.

## Who is eligible

Pass travel is a benefit for active employees after 90 days of continuous service. The
following people are enrolled pass riders once registered in the travel profile:

- the employee
- one spouse or registered domestic partner
- dependent children under age 26
- the employee's parents

Enrollment changes for pass riders are made by opening an HR ticket in the travel domain.

## Buddy passes

Each eligible employee receives 8 buddy passes per calendar year. Buddy passes let a
friend or relative who is not an enrolled pass rider travel on a space-available basis.

- Passes are issued on January 1. Employees hired during the year receive a prorated
  number: one pass for every six weeks remaining in the year, rounded down, at most 8.
- Unused buddy passes expire on December 31 and do not carry over.
- A buddy pass covers one person for one one-way segment. A round trip uses two passes.
- Buddy pass travelers pay a service charge per segment, set by route length:
  short haul under 750 miles, 30 dollars; medium haul 750 to 2,000 miles, 60 dollars;
  long haul over 2,000 miles, 90 dollars. Taxes and fees are extra.
- The employee is responsible for the conduct of every buddy pass traveler.
- Selling or trading buddy passes is prohibited and ends pass privileges.

## Boarding priority

Pass travelers board on a space-available basis after all revenue passengers. Among pass
travelers, priority follows the employee's seniority date. Enrolled pass riders board
with the employee's priority; buddy pass travelers board after all enrolled pass riders.

## Embargo periods

Pass travel, including buddy passes, is not permitted on embargo dates: the Sunday after
Thanksgiving, December 20 through January 3, and the Friday and Sunday of the Memorial
Day, Independence Day, and Labor Day weekends.

## Dress and conduct

Pass travelers dress neatly; beachwear, sleepwear, and clothing with offensive graphics
are not permitted. Crew instructions apply to pass travelers as to any customer.
