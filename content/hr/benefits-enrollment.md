# Benefits enrollment and life events

Synthetic policy for demonstration. Not a real employer's policy.

New employees enroll in medical, dental, and vision coverage within 31 days of their
hire date; coverage starts on the first day of the month after the hire date. Employees
who do not enroll in that window can enroll during open enrollment, which runs for two
weeks each November for coverage starting January 1.

## Qualifying life events

A marriage, a new domestic partnership, a birth or adoption, a divorce, or a loss of other
coverage allows changes outside open enrollment. The change is requested within 30 days
of the event by opening an HR ticket in the general domain. A move to a new home address
does not by itself allow a plan change unless the old plan is not offered at the new
address.
