# Paid time off and sick time

Synthetic policy for demonstration. Not a real employer's policy.

## Paid time off

Full-time employees accrue paid time off each pay period based on years of service:

| Years of service | Days per year | Hours per pay period |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Less than 5 | 15 | 4.62 |
| 5 to 14 | 20 | 6.15 |
| 15 or more | 25 | 7.69 |

Up to 40 hours of unused paid time off carries over into the next calendar year; the rest
is forfeited on December 31. Frontline employees bid for vacation periods by seniority
each autumn for the following year.

## Sick time

Employees accrue 1 hour of sick time for every 30 hours worked, up to 80 hours a year,
and may use it for their own illness or to care for a family member. Some states and
cities require more; where they do, the local rule applies.
