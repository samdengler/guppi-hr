# Pay schedule and pay statements

Synthetic policy for demonstration. Not a real employer's policy.

Employees are paid every two weeks, on Fridays, 26 times a year. A pay period runs 14
days from Sunday through Saturday, and its pay arrives on the Friday six days after the
period ends.

When a pay date falls on a bank holiday, pay arrives on the business day before it.

## Pay statements

A pay statement is available for every pay date. The HR Assistant lists the most recent
statements with the pay date, the period, gross pay, net pay, and the last four digits
of the account that received it. Full statements with each deduction line are in the
payroll portal.

## Pay questions and corrections

An employee who believes a paycheck is wrong opens an HR ticket in the pay domain with
the pay date. Missing pay or a shortfall of more than 200 dollars is handled the same
business day; other corrections are paid on the next regular pay date.
